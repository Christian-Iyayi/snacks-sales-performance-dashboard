# Beyond £2.3 Million in Sales: Uncovering the Real Profit Story

**Original report by Christian Iyayi**  
**Source:** [Medium article](https://medium.com/@krisnice2002gmail.com/beyond-2-3-million-in-sales-uncovering-the-real-profit-story-7bc34c3338dc)

## How can a snack business identify the products, regions, and customer trends that drive growth?

This question formed the foundation of my Snacks Sales Performance Dashboard. Using Power BI, I analyzed sales performance across categories, brands, and regions to uncover the factors influencing revenue, profitability, demand, and customer satisfaction. The objective was not only to understand what performed well, but also to explore how data can support more informed business decisions.

The dataset contains 200 sales records, representing 56,195 units sold, £277,652.03 in revenue, and £111,065.62 in profit. Overall, the business achieved a 40% profit margin, with an average selling price of £4.94 per unit.

By examining these performance patterns, businesses can make more strategic decisions around inventory management, regional expansion, and targeted sales initiatives.

All figures reflect the full reporting period contained in the dataset. Monetary values are presented in the currency provided in the source data.

## How the dashboard was developed

The analysis began by importing the Excel dataset into Power BI. Using Power Query, I cleaned and transformed the data, corrected data types, and established proper month sequencing for time-based analysis.

I then created several DAX measures to calculate key business metrics, including:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Units Sold
- Average Selling Price
- Profit Margin

The dashboard consolidates performance data across eight product categories, ten brands, and five regions. Interactive filters enable users to explore results by month and compare revenue, profit, volume, and margin from multiple perspectives.

This approach allows decision-makers to move beyond static reporting and investigate the drivers behind performance outcomes.

## 1. Comparing revenue performance with customer demand

The analysis revealed that Nuts was the highest revenue-generating category, delivering £51,084.92, equivalent to 18.4% of total revenue, from 7,183 units sold.

Chocolate followed with £44,566.56 in revenue (16.1% of total revenue), while Fruit Snacks generated £42,832.89 (15.4%).

Although Nuts generated the most revenue, Fruit Snacks recorded the strongest customer demand, selling 8,448 units, ahead of Chocolate at 7,991 units.

Combined, these three categories contributed approximately 50% of the business’s total revenue, highlighting their importance to overall performance.

Other categories also demonstrated strong market potential:

- Crackers: £29,902.60 revenue from 7,828 units
- Cookies: £29,878.95 revenue from 5,304 units
- Pretzels: £27,596.58 revenue from 6,791 units
- Chips: £27,094.46 revenue from 5,824 units
- Candy: £24,695.07 revenue from 6,826 units

### Business insight

Revenue alone does not provide a complete picture of success. Categories generating high sales value should be evaluated alongside categories with strong customer demand. Maintaining stock availability for leading product lines, monitoring replenishment cycles, and testing complementary product bundles may help increase both revenue and profitability while minimizing excess inventory.
